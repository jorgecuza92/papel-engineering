Changes made to OP_1:

- fonts replaced with Courier Prime Papel (20, 20 bold, 22 bold, 32 px);
- title shown lowercase in quotes, artist lowercase, and "hold" lowercase;
- bitrate, charging and file-type tags redrawn as 1px white outline boxes with lowercase text;
- volume bar in orange (#ff5000); the shuffle/repeat indicator replaced with the `[●]` mark;
- `papel-reel-paper`: every image recoloured to the papél palette (paper #f7efef, ink #000000, signal #ff5000, rule #b9b6af).
